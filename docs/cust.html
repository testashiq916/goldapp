1. Introduction & System Overview

GoldApp is a comprehensive web-based jewellery management system built for gold, silver, and platinum jewellery businesses. It manages the full business lifecycle — from buying raw material and manufacturing to selling, repair, and financial reporting.

What GoldApp Covers

Module CategoryWhat You Can Do
BillingSales bills, purchase bills, sales returns, purchase returns, diamond purchases
OrdersCreate and manage customer orders, advance collection, rate fixing, order-to-sale conversion
GoldsmithIssue/receive gold to goldsmiths, track weights, making charges, and balances
RefinerySend material for purification, receive back, track loss/wastage per refiner
Remake/RepairFull repair workflow — receive from customer → goldsmith → return to customer
BarcodeTag items with barcodes, scan during sales, manage counter stock, verify stock
StockItem master, group/subgroup, stock adjustments, ledger, period reports
DepositsParty weight deposits, partners deposit, interest calculation
Other ItemsBuy/sell non-precious goods (accessories, boxes, covers), maintain stock
Purity TestingRecord purity test results, issue purity certificates

AccountsReceipt, payment, journal, PDC, debit/credit notes, expense vouchers, billwise accounting
Reports200+ reports — sales, purchase, goldsmith, refinery, stock, customer, supplier, financial, tax
Browser Requirement: Use Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge for best results. The system works entirely in your browser — no software installation needed.

3. Sales Bill

The Sales Bill module records jewellery sales to customers. It automatically updates stock and can handle multiple items, discounts, taxes, making charges, and payments.

3.1 Creating a New Sales Bill

  1. Go to Transactions → Sales Bill → Bill
  2. The bill number is assigned automatically from the configured sequence
  3. Enter or search the Customer Code / Name (type a few letters and press Tab to search)
  4. Select the Bill Date and Bill Type (retail, wholesale, etc.)
  5. Select Counter and Salesman if applicable
  6. Add item rows one by one:
    • Enter Item Code or search by name
    • Fill Quantity, Gross Weight, Stone Weight
    • Enter Touch / Purity, Rate, Making Charge
    • Net Weight = Gross Weight − Stone Weight (auto-calculated)
    • Amount is auto-calculated from weight × rate + making charge
  7. Enter Discount amount or percentage if any
  8. Enter applicable Tax / GST details
  9. Enter Amount Received (cash / card / cheque)
  10. Click Save — the bill is printed / saved
Tip: Press Tab to move between fields quickly. Amounts auto-calculate as you type weights and rates.
Stock: Saving a sales bill reduces stock for each item sold. If an item has insufficient stock, the system will warn you.

Key Weight & Amount Fields

FieldDescription
Gross WeightTotal physical weight of the item including stones
Stone Weight (St.Wgt)Weight of embedded stones (deducted for pricing)
Net WeightGross Wgt − Stone Wgt — the chargeable gold weight
TouchGold purity % (e.g., 91.6 for 22 carat, 75.0 for 18 carat)
Touch WeightNet Weight × Touch ÷ 100 — pure gold equivalent
Making Charge (MC)Labour / manufacturing charge per gram or fixed amount
Stone PriceSeparate charge for the stone value
Mud / WastageWastage deduction amount
Less %Percentage discount on the item

3.2 Editing a Sales Bill

  1. Go to Transactions → Sales Bill → Edit
  2. Search by bill number, customer name, or date
  3. Select the bill to load it into edit mode
  4. Modify fields as needed
  5. Click Save — the system reverses the original stock entry and applies the corrected values
Important: Editing reverses the original stock and creates new stock movements. All linked accounting entries are also updated.

3.3 Cancelling a Sales Bill

  1. Go to Transactions → Sales Bill → Cancel
  2. Search and select the bill
  3. Confirm cancellation
Cancellation is permanent. Stock is reversed, accounting entries are removed. A cancelled bill cannot be recovered.

3.4 Reprinting a Sales Bill

Go to Transactions → Sales Bill → Reprint, search the bill, and print.

3.5 Bill Confirmation

If your business workflow requires a second-level approval before a bill is finalised, use Sales Bill → Bill Confirmation. Bills pending confirmation are listed; authorised users can confirm or reject them.

4. Sales Return

When a customer returns previously sold jewellery, record it as a Sales Return. Stock is added back and the customer's account is credited.

Creating a Sales Return

  1. Go to Transactions → Sales Return → Bill
  2. Enter the original Sales Bill number to load the bill details
  3. Select which items are being returned (partial returns are allowed)
  4. Verify the return weights and amounts
  5. Enter the amount refunded to the customer
  6. Save
Stock Update: Items returned are automatically added back to stock.

Edit, Cancel, and Reprint work the same way as for Sales Bills — use the corresponding sub-menu items under Sales Return.

5. Purchase Bill

Use Purchase Bill when buying jewellery, raw gold/silver, or other items from a supplier.

5.1 Creating a Purchase Bill

  1. Go to Transactions → Purchase Bill → Bill
  2. Enter Supplier Code / Name
  3. Enter Supplier's Bill Number (external reference) and Date
  4. Add item rows: Code, Name, Purity, Gross Weight, Stone Weight, Touch, Rate
  5. Enter HMC (Hallmark charge) if applicable
  6. Enter Discount and Tax details (GST / Tax External / Tax Deduct options)
  7. Enter TCS % if applicable — TCS Amount is auto-calculated
  8. Enter Amount Paid (cash / bank)
  9. Click Save

Purchase Amount Calculation

FieldFormula
Bill TotalSum of all item amounts
Net TotalBill Total − Discount − Exchange + HMC ± Tax/Cess
TCS AmountNet Total × TCS% ÷ 100 (rounded)
Final Net TotalNet Total + TCS Amount
BalanceFinal Net Total + Others − Amount Paid

Edit, Cancel, Reprint, and Confirmation work the same as Sales Bill — use sub-menu items under Purchase Bill.

6. Purchase Return

Use Purchase Return when sending back items to a supplier after purchase.

  1. Go to Transactions → Purchase Return → Bill
  2. Load the original Purchase Bill number
  3. Select items being returned
  4. Enter weights and amounts
  5. Save — stock is reduced and supplier's account is debited

7. Diamond / Precious Stone Purchase

A dedicated module for purchasing diamonds, precious stones, and similar items that require separate handling from gold/silver.

  1. Go to Transactions → Diamonds/Precious Stones → Purchase → New
  2. Enter Supplier, Date, and Supplier Bill Number
  3. Add stone items: stone type, weight (carats/grams), rate, amount
  4. Enter payment details and Save

Diamond Purchase Return is available under Diamonds → Purchase Return. Edit, Cancel, and Reprint are available as sub-menu items.

8. Order Management

Manage customer orders from creation through to delivery. Orders can be tracked, rate-fixed, blocked/unblocked, and converted to sales bills.

8.1 New Order

  1. Go to Transactions → Order → New Order (Ctrl+O)
  2. Enter Customer, Date, and required delivery date
  3. Add ordered items: description, weight, design requirements
  4. Enter Advance Amount collected from customer
  5. Save — an Order Number is generated

8.2 Order Workflow

8.4 Rate Fix

Use Order → Rate Fix to lock today's gold rate against a specific order. When the order is eventually converted to a sale, this fixed rate is used instead of the current market rate.

9. Goldsmith Transactions

Manage the flow of gold/silver/other metal to and from goldsmiths (karigar). Issue raw material and receive finished jewellery.

9.1 New Transaction

  1. Go to Transactions → Goldsmith → Transaction (Ctrl+G)
  2. Select Goldsmith from the list
  3. Choose Transaction Type: Issue (giving metal out) or Receipt (receiving work back)
  4. Enter Date and Document Number
  5. Add item rows: Item, Gross Weight, Stone Weight, Touch, Making Charge
  6. Save

9.2 New Work Note

Use Goldsmith → New Work Note (Ctrl+N) to issue a work instruction note to a goldsmith without a full transaction entry. This documents the design, weight, and requirements.

10. Refinery Module

Manages metal sent to and received from a refinery for purification, melting, or bar conversion.

10.1 New Refinery Entry (Forward)

  1. Go to Transactions → Refinery → New Entry (Ctrl+Y)
  2. Select Refiner from the list
  3. Enter Date and Document reference number
  4. Add item rows: Item description, Gross Weight, Touch (purity)
  5. Enter expected loss % (wastage)
  6. Save — status is set to Forward

10.2 Refinery Return (Receipt)

  1. Go to Transactions → Refinery → Returns (Ctrl+Shift+Y)
  2. Load the original forward entry
  3. Enter the Received Weight and Touch of the returned metal
  4. The system calculates actual loss vs expected loss
  5. Enter Making Charges and Payment details
  6. Save — status updates to Return
All In One: Use Refinery → All In One to record both forward and return in a single entry when the refinery transaction is settled immediately.

10.3 Refinery Reports

11. Remake / Repair Module

The Remake module handles the complete lifecycle of repair and remake work — receiving jewellery from a customer, sending to a goldsmith, receiving back, and returning to the customer with a final bill.

11.1 Receipt Memo To Party (Step 1)

When a customer brings jewellery for repair/remake:

  1. Go to Transactions → Remake → Rcpt Memo To Party → New
  2. Enter Customer Code / Name
  3. Set Receipt Date and Due Date (promised return date)
  4. Add item rows:
    • Item description, Quantity, Gross Weight, Stone Weight
    • Enter the Complaint / Work description (what needs to be done)
  5. Save — Receipt Bill Number is generated (e.g. RM1/00001)
Tip: Always set the Due Date accurately. The Remake Pending report highlights overdue items in red, helping staff follow up with goldsmiths.

11.2 Issue Memo To Smith (Step 2)

  1. Go to Transactions → Remake → Issue Memo To Smith → New
  2. Select the Goldsmith
  3. Reference the Receipt Memo bill number
  4. Add items issued with weights
  5. Save

11.3 Receipt Memo From Smith (Step 3)

  1. Go to Transactions → Remake → Rcpt Memo From Smith → New
  2. Select Goldsmith and reference the Issue Memo
  3. Enter the received weights (after work)
  4. Save

11.4 Issue Memo To Party (Step 4)

When returning the finished item to the customer without a final bill (interim return):

  1. Go to Transactions → Remake → Issue Memo To Party → New
  2. Load the original Receipt Memo
  3. Record items being returned
  4. Save

11.5 Repair Return — Final Bill (Step 5)

When collecting payment and issuing the final receipt to the customer:

  1. Go to Transactions → Remake → Repair Return → New
  2. Load the original Receipt Memo bill number
  3. Enter the new/final weight of the item after work
  4. Enter Making Charge, Bill Amount
  5. Enter Discount if applicable
  6. Enter Amount Received from the customer
  7. Save — Return Bill Number generated (e.g. RM4/00001)

11.6 Edit, Cancel, Reprint

Each of the 5 sub-modules (Rcpt Memo to Party, Issue to Smith, Rcpt from Smith, Issue to Party, Repair Return) has its own Edit, Cancel, and Reprint sub-menus. Use them the same way as for Sales Bill — search by bill number and select.

11.7 Remake Reports

Go to Reports → Remake → Remake Reports and select from the dropdown:

Report TypeWhat It Shows
Remake Entry DetailsAll items received from customers in the date range — customer, item, weight, complaint, status (Pending/Returned)
Goldsmith Issue DetailsAll items issued to goldsmiths — goldsmith name, item, weight, making charge, date
Goldsmith Rcpt DetailsAll items received back from goldsmiths — received weight, touch, making charge
Remake Return to Party DetailsAll items returned to customers — original bill reference, final weight, charge, amount collected

Remake Pending Report

Go to Reports → Remake → Remake Pending. This shows all items received from customers that have NOT yet been returned. Items past their Due Date are highlighted in red. No date filter is needed — it always shows all open items.

Remake Returns Report

Go to Reports → Remake → Remake Returns. Shows all final Repair Return bills in the selected date range — Bill No, Date, Customer, Original Bill, Net Weight, Bill Amount, Discount, Tax, Net Amount, Received, Balance.

12. Barcode Management

GoldApp supports full barcode-based inventory management — from tagging items to scanning during sales and verifying counter stock.

12.1 Barcode Single Entry

Assign a barcode to an individual item:

  1. Go to Transactions → BarcodeAdd → Single Entry
  2. Enter or scan the Barcode Number (or let the system generate the next one)
  3. Select the Item Code / Name
  4. Enter weight, purity, making charge, and other item details
  5. Assign to a Counter if applicable
  6. Save — the barcode is linked to this specific item
  7. Click Print to print the barcode label on your barcode printer

12.2 Barcode Multi Entry

Assign barcodes to multiple items at once (bulk tagging):

  1. Go to Transactions → BarcodeAdd → Multi Entry
  2. Select a source document (e.g., a purchase bill)
  3. The system lists all untagged items from that document
  4. Assign barcode numbers (auto-generated or manually entered) to each row
  5. Save all at once
  6. Print labels for all newly tagged items
Tip: Use Multi Entry after receiving a purchase bill to quickly tag all incoming stock.

12.3 Counter Issue

Transfer tagged items from main stock to a specific display counter:

  1. Go to Transactions → BarcodeAdd → Counter Issue
  2. Select the Counter you're issuing items to
  3. Scan or enter barcode numbers of items to be issued
  4. Save — stock moves from main inventory to the counter

12.4 Stock Verification

Verify physical items in a counter against the system's barcode records:

  1. Go to Transactions → BarcodeAdd → Stock Verification
  2. Select the Counter to verify
  3. Scan all items physically present in the counter
  4. The system shows which items match and highlights missing or extra items

12.5 Barcode Settings

Configure how barcodes are printed:

  1. Go to Transactions → BarcodeAdd → Single Entry → Settings
  2. Select the Printer connected to your system
  3. Upload a Label Template image if a custom design is required
  4. Set default fields to print on the label (item name, weight, price, barcode number)
  5. Save settings

12.6 Barcode Reports

Barcode Profit ReportMargin analysis: cost price vs selling price for barcode-tagged items

14. Weight Deposit Modules

14.1 Party Weight Deposit

Records gold/silver weight deposited by customers, smiths, or parties — and tracks withdrawals. Useful for parties who maintain a running gold balance with the shop.

Recording a Deposit or Withdrawal

  1. Go to Transactions → Party Wgt Deposit → Transaction
  2. Select the Party (customer / goldsmith / depositor)
  3. Choose type: Receipt (depositing) or Payment (withdrawing)
  4. Enter Date, Document Number, Metal Type, Weight
  5. Save

Opening Weight Entry

To set up a party's opening balance use Registration → Party Opening Weight.

Interest Posting

Use Party Wgt Deposit → Interest Posting to calculate and post interest on the running balance for all depositors at once.

Party Weight Deposit Reports

TransactionsAll deposit/withdrawal transactions in the date range; filter by party
Depositer LedgerRunning balance per party — opening + deposits − withdrawals per transaction
Wgt/Amt SummaryPer-party summary: total deposited, total withdrawn, balance weight and value

14.2 Partners Deposit (Weight)

Manages weight-based financial arrangements between the shop and its business partners (sleeping partners, gold loan providers, etc.).

New Partners Deposit Entry

  1. Go to Transactions → Partners Deposit (Wgt) → New (Ctrl+W)
  2. Select Partner
  3. Choose Receipt (R) — partner deposits gold — or Payment (P) — gold returned to partner
  4. Enter Date, Weight, Rate, Amount
  5. Save

Partners Deposit Reports

18. Accounts Module

The accounts module handles all financial entries — cash/bank receipts, payments, journal entries, cheque management, and billwise settlements.

18.1 Cash/Bank Receipt

  1. Go to Transactions → Accounts → Receipt (Alt+F2)
  2. Select Account (Cash / Bank)
  3. Enter Party Name, Date, Amount, and narration
  4. Save — credited to the party's account

18.2 Cash/Bank Payment

  1. Go to Transactions → Accounts → Payment (Alt+F3)
  2. Select Account, Party, Date, Amount
  3. Save — debited from the party's account

18.3 Customer Billwise Receipt

Collect payment against specific outstanding bills:

  1. Go to Accounts → Customer Billwise Rcpt (Alt+F1)
  2. Select Customer — all outstanding bills are listed
  3. Enter the amount paid against each bill
  4. Save — each bill's balance is reduced accordingly

18.4 Supplier Billwise Payment

Pay against specific outstanding supplier invoices:

  1. Go to Accounts → Supplier Billwise Payment
  2. Select Supplier — outstanding bills are listed
  3. Enter payment amounts per bill
  4. Save

18.5 Journal Entry

  1. Go to Accounts → Journal (Alt+J)
  2. Add debit and credit lines with account codes and amounts
  3. Debit total must equal Credit total
  4. Save

18.6 Daily Statement

Go to Accounts → Daily Statement (Ctrl+Alt+F2) to record the shop's daily cash position — opening balance, receipts, payments, closing balance.

18.7 Suspense Entry

Use Accounts → Suspense Entry (Ctrl+Shift+S) for transactions that cannot immediately be allocated to a specific account. The Suspense Master manages the temporary accounts used.

18.8 Post-Dated Cheques (PDC)

  1. Record received PDCs through the normal Receipt entry
  2. Go to Accounts → Cheque Clearance when the cheque is presented to the bank to clear it from suspense to the bank account
  3. View all PDCs via Reports → General → PDC Report

18.9 Debit / Credit Notes

Use Accounts → Debit/Credit Note to record adjustments, price differences, or corrections that are not full returns.

18.10 Expense Voucher

Go to Accounts → Expense Voucher to record shop expenses (rent, electricity, salaries, miscellaneous) against the appropriate expense account.

22. Tips, Shortcuts & Frequently Asked Questions

22.1 Data Entry Tips

  • Press Tab to move between fields without using the mouse
  • In search fields, type 3–4 characters and press Tab or Enter to trigger a popup search
  • Weight fields accept up to 3 decimal places (e.g., 10.750 grams)
  • Amount fields accept up to 2 decimal places
  • Always verify the bill total before saving — check discount and tax settings

22.2 Printing Tips

  • Use Ctrl+P or click Print in the module toolbar
  • For best print layout: set margins to Minimum, enable Background graphics
  • Use Reprint for any previously saved bill — no need to re-enter data
  • Reports can be saved as PDF using the browser's Save as PDF printer option

22.3 Frequently Asked Questions

Q: A bill was saved with wrong weight or amount. What do I do?

Go to the module's Edit sub-menu, search the bill, correct the values, and save. The system automatically reverses the original entry and applies corrected values.

Q: I accidentally cancelled a bill. Can I recover it?

No — cancellation is permanent and irreversible. Create a new bill with the same details.

Q: The report shows no data even though bills were entered.

Check: (1) the date range — it must include the bill dates; (2) you clicked Show after setting filters; (3) any party/item filter is not limiting the results unintentionally.

Q: How do I know if a remake job is overdue?

Go to Reports → Remake → Remake Pending. Overdue items (past due date) are highlighted in red.

Q: How is Balance calculated in reports?

Balance = Net Amount − Amount Received/Paid. Positive balance = customer owes you. Zero balance = fully settled.

Q: A barcode won't scan during sales. What to check?

(1) Ensure the barcode is tagged in Single Entry with a valid item. (2) Check the barcode number in Barcode Register. (3) Verify the scanner driver is connected and working. (4) Try manually entering the barcode number.

Q: Gold rate changed during the day. Which bills use which rate?

Each bill uses the rate that was active at the time the bill was entered. Updating the rate does not retroactively change saved bills.

Q: Can I export a report to Excel?

Right-click the report table → Select All → Copy → Paste into Excel. Or use Print → Save as PDF for a formatted document.

Q: The stock balance seems wrong. What to check?

(1) Run Integrity Checking to identify discrepancies. (2) Check the Stock Period Ledger for the item to see every movement. (3) Check if any bills were edited — edits reverse and repost stock. Contact your administrator if the issue persists.

Q: How do I add a new salesman?

Go to Registration → Account Masters → Sales Man — add the name and save. The new salesman will then be available in the Sales Bill dropdown.

Q: How do I set up a gold savings scheme for customers?

First create the scheme type in Registration → Scheme Type Master. Then enrol customers via Transactions → Scheme Details → Customer Registration. Collect monthly payments via Scheme Collection.

Q: Who can change user passwords or access rights?

Only users with administrator-level access can manage other users. They can do this via Registration → Users → Provide Access and Change Password.

Q: How do I print a customer's scheme passbook?

Go to Transactions → Scheme Details → Pass Book Print, select the customer and scheme, and print.

Q: The system says "Insufficient Stock" during a sales bill. What do I do?

The item's stock is zero or less than required. Check the Stock Period Ledger for the item to understand the current balance. You may need to enter a purchase bill or stock adjustment before proceeding.
